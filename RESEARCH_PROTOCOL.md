# Research protocol

1. Register the dataset, source contracts, hypothesis, universe, metrics,
   split periods, feature list, and search budget before substantive research.
2. Every attempt consumes one registry budget unit, including failed,
   interrupted, or discarded attempts. Attempt counts are never refunded.
3. Use chronological evaluation where event time matters. Keep decision-time
   inputs and future outcome labels physically and logically separate.
4. Do not query or expose protected labels to research code. Protected results
   are certificates/summary metrics only.
5. A model or LLM narrative cannot set a live probability without a versioned,
   tested, approved fusion artifact.
6. Keep PASS as a valid first-class decision and log every hard-gate failure.
7. No outcome experiment, edge search, odds-drift optimization, or live order
   placement is authorized in this foundation milestone.
8. Any strategy promotion requires a frozen artifact, reproducible inputs,
   protected or prospective confirmation, operational evidence, and explicit
   human approval before any live lifecycle.
