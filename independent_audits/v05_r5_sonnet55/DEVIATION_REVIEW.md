# Review of the 24 documented implementation deviations/interpretations

Source of the list: `adapters/evidence/S7/SUMMARY.md`, "Deviations and interpretations" (items 1–24), written by the
implementer at the S7 commit and **not** updated by R1–R5. Each item was re-read against
`V05_ADAPTER_ARCHITECTURE.md` at `c8dfafd` and, where behaviour was in question, re-tested with my own probe
(listed in the *Evidence* column; outputs under `evidence/`). The implementer's own classifications were not used.

Classification vocabulary: **CONFORMING INTERPRETATION** · **STRICTER BUT SAFE** · **AUTHORITY AMBIGUITY** ·
**UNAUTHORIZED SEMANTIC CHANGE** · **DEFECT**.

## Items the brief singled out

* **Formulas vs worked examples** (2): code takes the stricter of the two; wording in the summary is inaccurate; needs ratification.
* **Optional `startTime` / null price** (7): verified; conforming.
* **`bookmakerIsActive`** (6): new status level; fail-safe; needs an ADR before G3 (§10 rule 7).
* **Expected scope at `Tq`** (9): conforming after R3.
* **`complete_hint` / provider completeness** (10): authority ambiguity; residual false-ABSENT at fixture/bookmaker granularity.
* **`approve-ready` semantics** (13): conforming; time authority verified.
* **Coarse-clock handling** (18): conforming; Windows behaviour not reproducible here.
* **Reader strictness** (8): stricter but safe; verified.
* **Credential line endings** (19): stricter but safe.

No deviation was found to be an **UNAUTHORIZED SEMANTIC CHANGE** that loosens an invariant. Three are real defects
(3-partial, 11-partial, 24) and one is a test-quality defect (16); all are recorded in `FINDINGS.csv`.
